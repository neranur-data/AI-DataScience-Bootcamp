# CRM Analytics – Customer Segmentation & Lifetime Value

This folder contains hands-on CRM Analytics projects completed as part of my Data Science & AI Bootcamp using the FLO retail customer dataset.

## Projects

### 1. FLO RFM Analysis

**File:** `FLO_RFM.py`

Customer segmentation based on purchasing behavior using RFM analysis.

**Key concepts:**
- Recency: How recently a customer made a purchase.
- Frequency: How often a customer makes purchases.
- Monetary: How much a customer spends.

**Project objectives:**
- Explore and prepare customer transaction data.
- Calculate RFM metrics.
- Create customer segments based on purchasing behavior.
- Identify customer groups for targeted marketing strategies.

### 2. FLO Customer Lifetime Value Prediction

**File:** `FLO_CLTV_Prediction.py`

Predictive customer analytics focused on estimating future customer value.

**Project objectives:**
- Prepare and analyze historical customer purchase data.
- Examine customer purchasing frequency and monetary value.
- Estimate future customer purchasing behavior.
- Predict customer lifetime value (CLTV).
- Segment customers based on predicted value.

## Dataset

`flo_data_20k.csv` – FLO retail customer data used for both projects.
